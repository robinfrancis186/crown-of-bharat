# Crown app icon

Mode: built-in image_gen. Existing title artwork was used as a style reference. Original master: crown-icon-master.png. PNG sizes and ICO are resampled exports; the original is preserved.

Prompt:
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square mobile app icon and compact logo for Crown of Bharat. Reference image is style reference only: match its gold and emerald royal identity. Generate a single striking Indian royal crown emblem with a large emerald jewel and lotus-shaped crown points, bold sculpted gold silhouette, warm dimensional highlights, very restrained engraved detail. Centered on a deep emerald green opaque background filling the entire square edge to edge. Keep the entire important crown inside the central 70 percent of the square for app icon masks. Premium stylized 3D strategy-game art. Strong readable silhouette at 32px. No words, no letters, no title, no border, no rounded outer corners, no mockup, no additional objects. Square composition.

The result extends outside the requested mask-safe center, so the manifest intentionally declares these icons as `any`, not `maskable`. Google sign-in and connectivity remain required; adding a manifest does not add offline play or certify a native app-store build.
